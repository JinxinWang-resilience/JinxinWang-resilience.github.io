About Me

I am a postdoc working with Erick Delage while visiting Yuehaw Khoo at UChicago. I am passionate about methods (e.g., optimization, optimal transport, and decision-making under uncertainty) for real-world applications. I have worked on 1. nonconvex optimization for Cryo-EM/SLAM applications, 2. GPU-friendly 1st-order algorithms for nonlinear conic optimization problems, and 3. alignment/OT from data science, biology, and generative AI.

I received a PhD degree in Operations Research at CUHK in Dec 2024 advised by Anthony Man-Cho So. I also get advice from Huifu Xu and Viet Anh Nguyen.

I obtained an MPhil degree in Data Science (2017-2020) from Peking University supervised by Zaiwen Wen, and a bachelor's degree in Applied Mathematics (2013-2017; Loo-Keng Hua Elite Program; minor in Finance) from Dalian University of Technology. In addition, I was an exchange student (2016-2017) under the supervision of Xin Liu at the Academy of Mathematics and Systems Science (AMSS), the Chinese Academy of Sciences (CAS).
